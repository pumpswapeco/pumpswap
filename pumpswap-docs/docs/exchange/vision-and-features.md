# Hybrid Exchange Vision

Pumpswap's long-term product vision is a next-generation hybrid cryptocurrency exchange that merges the speed and user experience of a centralized exchange (CEX) with the transparency, self-custody, and on-chain settlement of decentralized infrastructure (DEX).

The hybrid exchange is a *planned product* targeting Q2 2027. It is separate from the Pumpswap staking platform, which is live on Solana Devnet and entering security audit.

This page describes our product direction. Features are subject to implementation, audit, and regulatory review.


## Product Vision

**Why Hybrid?**

Centralized exchanges are fast but opaque. Decentralized exchanges are transparent but slow and complex.

Pumpswap will deliver the best of both:

**CEX Performance + DEX Transparency = Hybrid Advantage**

Our goal is to become the foundational trading layer where staking, trading, and liquidity converge for the Solana ecosystem and beyond


## Planned Features


### Pro Trading Tools

A professional-grade trading experience designed for both retail and institutions:

- Real-time market data, advanced charting (TradingView integration), and depth visualization
- Spot trading with limit, market, stop-loss, and TWAP orders
- Portfolio management, P&L analytics, and trade history
- Low-latency matching engine targeting &lt;50ms order execution

### Security-First Architecture

Security is not a feature, it is our foundation. Our architecture evaluation includes:

- **Multi-Party Computation (MPC) Wallets** for institutional-grade custody with no single point of failure
- **Smart-Contract Settlement** for transparent, on-chain proof of trades
- **Hybrid Custody Model:** Users choose between self-custody and secure MPC custody
- Cold wallet storage, withdrawal whitelists, and real-time threat monitoring
- Independent security audits and bug bounty program before mainnet

### Blockchain and Smart-Contract Integration

Where blockchain adds real value, we use it:

- On-chain settlement and verifiable proof-of-reserves
- Staking-integrated trading: Stake directly from your trading balance
- SPL Token and Token-2022 native support for instant listings
- Cross-program composability with Pumpswap staking pools

All smart contracts will undergo rigorous testing, audit, and formal verification before production.


### Ecosystem Token Utility: LP & PLH

The hybrid exchange will be powered by the Pumpswap ecosystem tokens:

- **PLH Utility:** Up to 50% trading fee discounts, staking rewards up to 30% APY, launchpad access, and governance
- **LP Utility:** Legacy benefits for early supporters, fee sharing, and priority listing access
- **Buyback & Burn Engine:** 30% of exchange fees used for PLH buyback and burn
- **Liquidity Mining:** Deep PLH/SOL and PLH/USDC pools with incentivized rewards

Token utility and fee structures will be finalized and documented prior to exchange launch.

### Automated Strategies & Copy Trading

Making pro trading accessible to everyone:

- DCA bots, Grid bots, and Smart Rebalance tools
- Copy trading: Follow top-performing traders with on-chain transparency
- Strategy marketplace for creators to monetize their strategies
- Advanced risk controls: max drawdown limits, stop-loss enforcement

### Project Listings & Launchpad

Pumpswap will be the go-to venue for blockchain projects:

- *Fast-Track Listings* for projects using Pumpswap staking infrastructure
- *Pumpswap Launchpad:* Fair launch platform for vetted Solana projects with PLH staking access
- Transparent listing criteria: security audit, liquidity commitment, community traction
- No listing fees for ecosystem partners during genesis phase
