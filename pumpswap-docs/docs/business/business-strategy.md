# Business Strategy

## Product Direction

Pumpswap is developing a cryptocurrency ecosystem with two principal product directions:

1. **Solana staking infrastructure** â€” a non-custodial platform designed to help Solana project founders launch and manage staking pools without building all the infrastructure themselves.
2. **Hybrid cryptocurrency exchange** â€” a longer-term product initiative currently targeted for Q2 2027.

These products are at different stages of development. The staking platform has been deployed and tested on Solana Devnet, while the hybrid exchange remains a planned initiative.

## Current Priority: Staking Infrastructure

Pumpswap's staking platform focuses on configurable pools, staking and reward management, token compatibility, NFT boosts, and administrative controls.

The platform supports both the original SPL Token Program and Token-2022. Both token standards have been tested successfully on Solana Devnet.

Current priorities include:

- Expanding integration and edge-case testing.
- Validating staking, unstaking, reward funding, and reward claims.
- Maintaining compatibility with supported SPL Token Program and Token-2022 configurations.
- Reviewing NFT boost verification and reward calculations.
- Verifying administrative permissions, pause functionality, and emergency controls.
- Improving reliability, error handling, and developer documentation.
- Preparing for an independent security audit.
- Evaluating requirements for eventual mainnet deployment.

Successful Devnet testing is an important development milestone, but it does not replace an independent security audit or establish mainnet production readiness.

## Longer-Term Exchange Direction

The planned Pumpswap hybrid exchange is intended to broaden the ecosystem beyond staking infrastructure.

Its development will require separate technical planning, security reviews, operational preparation, and assessment of applicable legal and compliance requirements.

The current Q2 2027 target is a development objective, not a guaranteed launch date.

See [Hybrid Exchange Vision and Features](/docs/exchange/vision-and-features) for more information.

## Security and Sustainable Growth

Pumpswap prioritizes staged development, technical verification, and transparent communication.

Key priorities include:

- Preparing for an independent security audit.
- Addressing identified vulnerabilities before mainnet deployment.
- Maintaining automated tests and token-program compatibility checks.
- Documenting supported functionality and known limitations.
- Evaluating operational monitoring and incident-response procedures.
- Gathering feedback from developers and potential project partners.
- Clearly distinguishing deployed, tested, planned, and independently verified functionality.

## Funding and Partnerships

Pumpswap is positioned for sustainable growth through strategic funding, high-impact partnerships, and ecosystem expansion.

## Funding Strategy:

Pumpswap is actively engaged in securing strategic funding to accelerate development, security audits, and mainnet deployment. Our funding approach prioritizes long-term ecosystem partners over short-term capital, with a focus on investors who bring technical value, liquidity support, and market access. All funding milestones will be publicly disclosed with verifiable on-chain evidence upon confirmation.

## Strategic Partnerships:

We are in active discussions with leading Solana infrastructure providers, staking protocols, launchpads, and market makers to establish the Pumpswap Liquidity Network. Our partnership pipeline includes:

- Solana ecosystem projects seeking no-code staking solutions
- Audit firms for independent security verification
- Liquidity providers and market makers to support PLH at listing
- CEX and DEX partners for broad distribution and accessibility
- KOL and community networks for global ecosystem growth

## Exchange Listings & Adoption:

PLH is being prepared for listings on top-tier decentralized exchanges (Raydium, Jupiter) followed by a strategic rollout to leading centralized exchanges. Our listing strategy is backed by locked liquidity, transparent tokenomics, and a deflationary buyback-and-burn model designed to support long-term value.

## Why Partners Choose Pumpswap:
Unlike single-product protocols, Pumpswap offers a dual growth engine: immediate utility through our staking infrastructure and long-term scalability through our hybrid exchange vision. Partners gain early access to a platform that solves a real problem for hundreds of Solana projects - staking infrastructure - while aligning with a future exchange ecosystem.

We are building partnerships based on transparency, technical competence, and shared growth. All partnerships, listings, and funding achievements will be announced with official documentation and verifiable proof.

Our Commitment: Every milestone will be backed by evidence, not promises.
