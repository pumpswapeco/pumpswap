# Pumpswap Liquidity Hub (PLH) Tokenomics

Pumpswap Liquidity Hub (PLH) is the next-generation liquidity and rewards layer for the Pumpswap ecosystem. By leveraging advanced Solana staking infrastructure, PLH connects projects, stakers, and liquidity providers seamlessly, ensuring deep liquidity, reduced slippage, and sustainable rewards.
This document describes the supply, allocation, vesting, and utility model for PLH.

> **Status:** PLH launch and token mechanisms are subject to implementation and verification. Token allocations, locks, rewards, and launch controls.

## 5. Utility

Potential utility includes:

- **Staking rewards:** Eligible users may stake PLH to receive rewards under published program rules and available reward budgets.
- **Platform incentives:** PLH will be used for community campaigns, referrals, and ecosystem participation.
- **Potential fee benefits:** PLH may provide discounts or other benefits on planned Pumpswap platform services.
- **Ecosystem participation:** Governance or community-voting mechanisms will be introduced  with clear rules.

## 7. Treasury and Governance Controls

The treasury allocation is 300 million PLH. Potential controls include:

- Multisignature wallet custody.
- Published treasury wallet addresses and balances.
- Transparent records of material treasury transactions.
- Community voting for specified discretionary expenditures.
- Periodic reporting on spending and remaining allocations.


## 8. Supply and Fee Mechanisms

Potential future mechanisms under consideration include using a portion of platform fees for PLH buybacks and burns, and conducting periodic supply-reduction events.


## 9. Launch Protections

Potential launch measures include:

- Clearly published sale and liquidity wallets.
- Transparent, verifiable vesting and liquidity locks.
- Anti-bot or anti-sniper controls, if implemented and tested.
- Documented transaction limits, if used.
- Public disclosure of mint and freeze authorities.
