# LiquidPump and Pumpswap Liquidity Hub (PLH)

## Overview

The Pumpswap ecosystem consists of two core assets with distinct roles: Live Solana staking infrastructure and a planned hybrid cryptocurrency exchange. LiquidPump (LP) and Pumpswap Liquidity Hub (PLH) are separate tokens with different statuses and utility.

### Potential LiquidPump utility

The following uses are proposed for future ecosystem services.

- **Trading fee benefits:** Potential fee discounts on Pumpswap exchange services.
- **Staking and rewards:** Potential reward programs, subject to implemented rules and available funding.
- **Early access:** Potential access to eligible future listings or token-launch opportunities.
- **Governance:** Potential participation in ecosystem decisions if a governance mechanism is introduced.

## 2. Pumpswap Liquidity Hub (PLH)

PLH is a next-generation liquidity and rewards layer designed to provide robust liquidity solutions for the entire Pumpswap ecosystem. By leveraging advanced staking technology, PLH connects projects, stakers, and liquidity seamlessly, ensuring deep liquidity pools and reducing trading slippage.



### Token parameters

| Network | Solana |

| Maximum supply target | 5,000,000,000 PLH |
| Initial circulation target | 2,000,000,000 PLH (40%) |

### PLH allocation

The allocation of the 5 billion PLH supply is:

| Category | Allocation | Tokens |
|---|---:|---:|
| Public sale | 36% | 1,800,000,000 |
| Liquidity and exchange-listing reserve | 20% | 1,000,000,000 |
| Community rewards, staking, and airdrops | 15% | 750,000,000 |
| Team and founders | 10% | 500,000,000 |
| Marketing and partnerships | 9% | 450,000,000 |
| Treasury and ecosystem fund | 6% | 300,000,000 |
| Advisors and KOLs | 4% | 200,000,000 |
| **Total** | **100%** | **5,000,000,000** |


### Initial circulation target

The 2 billion PLH initial circulation supply consists of:

- **1.8 billion PLH:** Public-sale allocation.
- **200 million PLH:** Initial liquidity, sourced from the 1 billion PLH liquidity and exchange-listing allocation.


### Utility and rewards

Potential PLH utility includes:

- PLH staking rewards under published program rules.
- Community incentives, airdrops, and referrals.
- Potential fee benefits on planned platform services.
- Potential ecosystem participation or governance if implemented.

A community allocation of 750 million PLH will be subdivided into LP staking rewards paid in PLH, PLH staking rewards, and community incentives. 


### Vesting and transparency

The current proposal includes a six-month cliff followed by 18 months of linear vesting for the team allocation, and separate proposed schedules for community, marketing, and advisor allocations.
