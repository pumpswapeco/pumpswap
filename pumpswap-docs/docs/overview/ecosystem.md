# Pumpswap Ecosystem

The Pumpswap ecosystem is a self-reinforcing flywheel bringing together a live Solana staking platform, a planned hybrid exchange, and powerful tokenomics via LiquidPump and PLH.

Each component has a distinct role, but together they create one powerful growth engine.

The Flywheel: How It All Connects

**LiquidPump (Community) → PLH (Utility & Rewards) → Staking Platform (Product) → Hybrid Exchange (Revenue) → Back to Community**

This is not 3 separate products. It's one ecosystem where every part drives value to the other.


## Pumpswap Staking Platform

**Status: Live on Devnet | Entering Audit | Mainnet Q1 2027**

The core infrastructure for Solana project founders who want to configure and manage non-custodial staking pools without building from scratch.

What it delivers today:

- Pool configuration with flexible APY, lock periods, and reward management
- Reward funding, staking, unstaking, and instant claim operations
- NFT boost functionality for gamified staking and community engagement
- Administrative controls: Role-based access, emergency pause, and freeze safeguards
- Full compatibility: SPL Token Program + Token-2022 with all extensions verified

**Traction:** Public GitHub, 100+ test cases, Devnet live, audit preparation complete.

**Business Model:** 2% fee on reward distributions + premium features for pro projects - sustainable revenue from day one.


## Planned Hybrid Exchange

 Planned Hybrid Exchange - THE REVENUE LAYER

**Status: Planned - Target Q2 2027**

Pumpswap's longer-term vision is a hybrid cryptocurrency exchange that will become the monetization layer for the entire ecosystem.

**Why it matters for investors:**

- Converts staking users into traders
- 30% of trading fees fund PLH buyback & burn
- Provides liquidity and price discovery for PLH and all staked tokens
- Fast-track listings for projects using Pumpswap staking = built-in pipeline of 100+ tokens

**Target:** Q2 2027 Private Beta -> Public Beta -> Full Launch Q3 2027. All timelines subject to audit and security validation.

## Pumpswap Liquidity Hub (PLH)

Pumpswap Liquidity Hub (PLH) - THE VALUE ACCRUAL TOKEN

*Status: Upcoming - TGE Q1 2027 - Tokenomics Finalized*

Pumpswap Liquidity Hub (PLH) is the utility and rewards token that powers the entire ecosystem.

*Fixed Supply: 5 Billion | Mint & Freeze Authority Revoked at TGE | Deflationary*

PLH Utility:

- **Trading:** Up to 50% fee discounts on hybrid exchange
- **Staking:** Up to 30% APY for staking PLH, governance rights, launchpad access
- **Deflationary Engine:** 30% of all platform fees (staking + exchange) used for PLH buyback & burn
- **Ecosystem Rewards:** Dedicated reward pool for LiquidPump holders - staking LP earns PLH
- **Locked & Verifiable:** Team 500M locked 6M cliff + 18M linear via PinkLock, public verification

PLH is not just a coin - it is the share of the entire Pumpswap revenue engine.


## Development Principles

1.  **Security First, Always:** No mainnet without audit. No audit without full test coverage. We will not compromise speed for security.
2.  **Build in Public:** Public GitHub, public roadmap updates, public vesting locks - transparency is our marketing
3.  **Non-Custodial by Design:** Users control their assets. We build tools, not custody traps
4.  **Composability:* Built for SPL and Token-2022 from day one - future-proof for Solana's next 10 years
5.  **Sustainable Economics:** Real fees, real buyback, real utility - not ponzi rewards
