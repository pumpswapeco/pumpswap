# Introduction to Pumpswap

Pumpswap is a hybrid cryptocurrency exchange ecosystem building the foundational infrastructure for trading, staking, and liquidity on Solana.

Our mission is to simplify what is complex. We combine a secure hybrid exchange with a no-code staking platform that allows any Solana project founder to create and manage staking pools without building the underlying infrastructure from scratch.


## The Pumpswap Ecosystem

The Pumpswap ecosystem is built on three core pillars that feed each other:

### 1. Solana Staking Platform - FLAGSHIP - LIVE ON DEVNET

Our flagship product, already live and battle-tested on Solana Devnet.

A non-custodial, no-code staking platform that enables any Solana project founder to:

- Launch configurable staking pools in under 5 minutes
- Manage flexible APY, reward funding, and distribution with one dashboard
- Support both SPL Token Program and Token-2022 with all extensions
- Deliver NFT-based staking boosts and real-time analytics to their community
- Deploy with audited smart contracts - no coding required

**Problem Solved:** 500+ new Solana tokens launch monthly. 90% cannot afford to build secure staking infrastructure. Pumpswap solves this in minutes.

**Status:** Live on Devnet | Entering Independent Audit | Mainnet Q4 2026


### 2. Hybrid Exchange - COMING Q2 2027

A next-generation hybrid exchange that merges the speed and user experience of a centralized exchange (CEX) with the transparency and self-custody of decentralized infrastructure (DEX).

**CEX Performance + DEX Transparency = Hybrid Advantage**

- 50ms matching engine with TradingView charts and pro trading tools
- MPC-based custody + on-chain settlement with proof-of-reserves
- Smart-contract integrated staking: Trade and stake from one balance
- 50% fee discounts for PLH holders, 30% of fees used for PLH buyback & burn

**Status:** Architecture Finalization Q1 2027 | Private Beta Q2 2027 | Public Beta Q2 2027


### 3. LiquidPump and Pumpswap Liquidity Hub

LiquidPump is the gateway to the Pumpswap ecosystem - live and community-driven.

Pumpswap Liquidity Hub (PLH) is the utility and rewards token powering the entire ecosystem:

- **Utility:** Fee discounts (up to 50%), staking rewards (up to 30% APY), governance, launchpad access
- **Deflationary:** 30% of all platform fees used for buyback & burn, fixed supply of 5B with mint authority revoked
- **Rewards:** Dedicated reward initiative for LiquidPump (LP) holders
- **Locked:** Team tokens locked 6M cliff + 18M vesting, verifiable on-chain

**Status:** Tokenomics Finalized | PLH TGE Q4 2026 | Pre-Audit
